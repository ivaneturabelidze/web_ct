Open Georgian Sea Gates & Find your Golden Fleece

At the very Eastern edge of the Black Sea, the blazing surface is crossed by beautiful, coastal landscapes dominated by the white peaks of the Caucasus Mountain Range, green forested hills, plantations of citruses and tea. This is Georgia, the country of an amazing puzzle of cultures, fascinating landscapes, and ancient history; it was here where the ship of the Argonauts docked to reach the legendary Colchis in search of the Golden Fleece.
