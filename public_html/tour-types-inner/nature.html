Georgia’s natural beauty lies in the variety of its landscapes, topographical relief, and climatic zones. Visitors are awed by the region’s abundance of unique plant and animal life, high rates of endemism, and breathtaking vistas of remote glaciers, canyons cut deep by rivers, wetland ecosystems, waterfalls, grottos, and speleological caves.
